Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your email address when you sign in using Google Auth. We also collect telemetry data generated by your vehicle (via OBD-II Bluetooth) and location data (GPS) strictly for providing the features of the DriveLogic AI application.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your vehicle and trip data are primarily stored locally on your device to ensure maximum privacy and minimize cloud storage costs. We use your authentication data (via Firebase) strictly to manage your subscription tier and remaining AI diagnostic tokens.

3. Google Drive Integration

If you choose to use our Cloud Backup feature, DriveLogic AI will request permission to access your Google Drive.

  • Data Created by Our App: We only read, write, and delete files inside your Google Drive that were explicitly created by DriveLogic AI.
  • No General Access: We do not have access to read, modify, or delete any of your personal files, photos, or documents stored in Google Drive.
  • Data Security: The backups consist of your trip logs and maintenance records encoded in standard JSON formats and are transmitted securely via Google's official APIs.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer to outside parties your Personally Identifiable Information or vehicle data. Certain anonymized data may be processed via AI engines (e.g., Google Gemini) solely to generate diagnostic reports as requested by you.

5. Data Retention

Your primary data is stored on your local device and can be deleted by you at any time by clearing the app data or using the in-app delete features. Cloud backups on Google Drive remain under your full control and can be deleted from within the app or directly from your Google Drive interface.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our developer email provided on the Google Play Store.
